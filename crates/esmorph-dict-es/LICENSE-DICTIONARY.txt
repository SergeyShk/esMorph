The dictionary of esMorph (es.dict) is built from:

- the Spanish entries of the English Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), by the Wiktionary
  contributors, from the dump of 2026-10-01 as extracted by wiktextract (https://kaikki.org;
  snapshot: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SergeyShk/esmorph-wiktionary), licensed under the
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0);
- the table of closed classes (data/closed-classes.tsv), reviewed after UD Spanish AnCora, and the
  counts of analyses from its training part (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Spanish-AnCora),
  release 2.18, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0);
- the counts of parts of speech from the training part of UD Spanish GSD
  (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Spanish-GSD), release 2.18, licensed under the Creative
  Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0).
